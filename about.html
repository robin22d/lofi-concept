About LOFI

A small workshop in Cardigan, West Wales

LOFI Design Ltd is a small startup making outdoor kit that's light, clever and built to be repaired. It started with one question: could a wood fire be as easy as gas?

Meet Jonny

Jonny grew up in Somerset and has been designing things for most of his working life. He designed halogen bike lights, worked on micro-hydro installations in Wales, and helped develop a digital Braille display.

The LOFI fire project started during the 2020 lockdown. A few prototypes later, it became the LOFI Stove, and then the Stove 2.0.

In the workshop

Parts are pressed on a hydraulic press with custom tooling, riveted, drilled and finished by hand on CNC and bench drills. The welded titanium parts and circuit boards come from specialist suppliers in China, then everything is processed and assembled here in Wales.

How it got here

  1. 2020

    The fire project starts in lockdown.

  2. The first LOFI Stove

    Titanium, fan-assisted, powered over USB.

  3. June 2025

    LOFI Stove 2.0 launches: 150 grams, with a fan you control from your power bank.

  4. 2026

    The kit grows: the Snips, the Oat Milk Tin and the Big Cup.

Made by hand in West Wales

Every LOFI Stove 2.0 is assembled in the workshop in Cardigan and backed by free repairs for a year.
